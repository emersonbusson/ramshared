## Resumo
Refactored connection handshake loops and message processing in `crates/ramshared-wsl2d/src/conn.rs` to replace deeply nested `match` and `if/else` constructs with `let-else` guard clauses. This improves readability and adheres to the Guard Clauses Clean Architecture principle. Restored semantic error logging within the guards to ensure observability is maintained without swallowing failure contexts.
