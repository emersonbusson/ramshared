### Evaluation Categories:

- **ACCEPT (Valid Directly)**: PRs with clean implementation, zero residue, preserving semantics, enhancing readability or robustness, and passing tests.

- **REJECT (Invalid / Rejected)**: PRs that are conceptually flawed, heuristic, or violate virtualization/WSL2 invariants.
